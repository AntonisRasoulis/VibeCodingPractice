How to play

Red (top screen)

W jump · S use lightning / hand

A D run left / right

Blue (bottom screen)

↑ jump · ↓ use lightning / hand

← → run left / right

Both runners race through the exact same course. Keep running right — a wall of fire is chasing each of you, and it gets faster and faster. If the fire catches you, the other player wins.

  • Spikes and pits — touch one and you'll come back a little way behind after a second. That's time the fire uses to catch up.
  • Moving platforms — slide back and forth over big gaps. Hop on and ride across.
  • Crumbling platforms — shake when you land on them, then fall. Keep moving!
  • Portals — a blue portal on a high ledge teleports you to the orange one ahead, skipping a spiky stretch.
  • Spike shooters — stone blocks that fire 4 spikes diagonally every couple of seconds. They sit on the ground, float at head height or hang overhead. Their red core glows just before they fire, so time your run.
  • Missiles — the rarest danger, after 300 m. One zooms in from far away in the background, from the left or the right, then locks on to the closest player: a red target flashes on them and a dotted line shows where it will fly. Then it fires in a straight line. Black missiles aim where you are; red-striped ones (after 400 m) aim where you're going to be, so change your speed or jump. Stay clear of the explosion.
  • Swinging maces — spiked balls swinging on chains from the sky, each at its own speed. Run past while it's swung away, or jump right over it.
  • Golden orbs — floating in the air. Some swap you and the other player's places. Others freeze you for 3 seconds. They look almost the same, so look closely before you jump into one. Each orb only works once.
  • Dash orbs — golden orbs with a » in the middle. Grab one and you hang in the air for a second, then get launched far to the right. Where you land is up to luck.
  • Lightning orbs — golden orbs with a white zigzag. Grab one to store a lightning shot, then press S (Red) or ↓ (Blue) to fire it along the ground. It flies the way you're facing when you shoot, so turn around (press left) to shoot back at a player behind you. It travels to the other player wherever they are. Jump over it, or get zapped back.
  • Hand orbs — golden orbs with a purple hand. Grab one, then press S (Red) or ↓ (Blue) to throw a mystic hand a short way in the direction you're facing. Facing right, it grabs the other player and suplexes them backwards. Facing left, it backhands them further back. It flies in a straight line, so jump over it or duck under it. You hold one power at a time: a new lightning or hand orb replaces the old one.

Press P or Esc to pause. Press F or the Fullscreen button to make the game fill the screen.

A practice project. The course is generated as you play, so every race is different and it never ends.
